## 1. A3 acceptance of A1 §6 conditions

| # | A1 condition | A3 acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `dma_constraints` fills `addr_mask` as a contiguous low-bit mask, `max_segment` as bytes per physically contiguous segment, and `coherent` truthfully; A1 never infers them from a device class | **Accepted.** A3 supplies device-derived values; no class-based inference. |
| 2 | `dma_quiesce` stops new DMA, waits for in-flight requests, is idempotent, and does not free A1 buffers | **Accepted.** A3 implements this callback and does not free buffers in quiesce. |
| 3 | `reset` runs only after quiesce and after requests complete or are cancelled; it does not free A1 DMA buffers or unmap BARs; A3 reinitialises | **Accepted.** A1 owns the full device reset; A3 reinitialises and re-arms its queue. |
| 4 | `io_submit` does not complete before `o0_io_submit` returns; synchronous completion is deferred | **Accepted.** Completion is deferred to IRQ, poll or process context after return. |
| 5 | `io_cancel` is best-effort; `O0_EBUSY` means completion will still run | **Accepted.** A3 will not treat `O0_EBUSY` as a guarantee that the callback will not run. |
| 6 | BARs are accessed only through `o0_device_map_bar`, as UC/NX mappings, never through the HHDM | **Accepted.** A3 will not derive BAR addresses from the HHDM. |
| 7 | A transfer larger than `max_segment` splits into multiple `o0_dma_buffer` objects and multiple `o0_io_request` objects; no SG pointer is added | **Accepted.** `o0_bio` embeds one request; large transfers are split. |
| 8 | Stage 1 DMA is trusted ring-0 only; no userspace or untrusted driver DMA before an IOMMU domain exists | **Accepted for Stage 1.** A3 claims no IOMMU isolation. |
